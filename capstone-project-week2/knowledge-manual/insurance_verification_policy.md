# AfyaPlus Insurance Verification Policy

## Overview

AfyaPlus Health provides tiered medical insurance cover for members in Kenya. This manual defines verification rules for outpatient, dental, and maternity benefits.

## Cover Tiers

### Bronze Tier
- Outpatient visits: covered up to KES 15,000 per year after a 30-day waiting period.
- Dental checkups: one routine checkup per year; extractions require pre-authorization.
- Maternity: not included.

### Silver Tier
- Outpatient visits: covered up to KES 50,000 per year after a 14-day waiting period.
- Dental: routine cleaning and one filling per year; orthodontics excluded.
- Maternity: antenatal visits covered after 90-day waiting period; delivery capped at KES 80,000.

### Gold Tier
- Outpatient visits: covered up to KES 120,000 per year; no waiting period for renewals.
- Dental: full preventive and restorative care; implants require pre-authorization.
- Maternity: full antenatal and delivery cover up to KES 200,000 after 60-day waiting period.

## Verification Workflow

1. Confirm member ID format `AP-######` is active in the AfyaPlus registry.
2. Match the requested service to the member's tier and waiting-period status.
3. For dental claims, verify whether the procedure is routine (covered) or surgical (pre-auth required).
4. Document the verification outcome with policy section reference before routing to billing or clinical teams.

## Outpatient Dental Rules

- Routine dental checkups are covered under Silver and Gold tiers only.
- Bronze members must pay out of pocket for dental services unless upgraded.
- Emergency dental pain with swelling may be routed to urgent care; coverage still follows tier limits.

## Pre-Authorization

Pre-authorization is required for:
- Dental extractions and surgical procedures
- Maternity delivery (Silver and Gold)
- Any outpatient visit exceeding KES 25,000 estimated cost

Members must submit pre-auth at least 48 hours before the scheduled procedure.

## Claims Timeline

Approved outpatient claims are reimbursed within 14 business days. Dental claims with pre-authorization are processed within 21 business days.

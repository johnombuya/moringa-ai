# AfyaPlus Clinical Routing Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol guides clinical routing for member inquiries received through the AfyaPlus verification desk. Routing decisions must be grounded in documented symptoms and policy-eligible pathways.

## Triage Categories

### Emergency (Route to Emergency Services)
Immediate escalation when any of the following red flags are present:
- Severe chest pain with shortness of breath
- Sudden difficulty speaking or facial drooping (stroke signs)
- Uncontrolled bleeding
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe allergic reaction with airway swelling

Emergency routing bypasses insurance waiting periods. Document the red-flag criteria used.

### Urgent Care (Same-Day Appointment)
Route when symptoms require prompt attention but are not life-threatening:
- High fever above 39°C lasting more than 24 hours in adults
- Persistent vomiting preventing fluid intake
- Moderate asthma exacerbation with wheezing
- Suspected fracture without open wound

### Primary Care (Routine Appointment)
Route for non-urgent concerns:
- Prescription renewals for stable chronic conditions
- Mild fever in otherwise well children
- Routine follow-up after discharged outpatient care
- Insurance verification questions without acute symptoms

### Specialist Referral
Refer when primary care cannot manage within scope:
- Recurrent dental abscess requiring oral surgery (requires pre-auth)
- High-risk pregnancy beyond antenatal visit scope
- Uncontrolled diabetes requiring endocrinology review

## Routing Documentation

Every routing decision must record:
1. Detected symptoms or inquiry type
2. Red-flag assessment (yes/no with criteria)
3. Recommended destination (Emergency Services, Urgent Care, Primary Care, Specialist)
4. Policy reference if insurance verification is involved

## Mombasa Regional Escalation

For Mombasa coastal clinics (`HOSP-########` format), after-hours emergency calls route to Mombasa County Referral Hospital partner network. Urgent cases during business hours route to the nearest AfyaPlus coastal hub.

## Medication Volume Guidance

When calculating liquid medication volumes for dispensing:
- Use prescribed dose in milligrams, concentration (mg/mL), doses per day, and duration in days.
- Always verify concentration before finalizing volume to avoid dosing errors.

## Out-of-Scope Inquiries

Questions unrelated to AfyaPlus insurance, clinical routing, or compliance policy must be declined. Do not provide travel, tourism, or general lifestyle advice.
